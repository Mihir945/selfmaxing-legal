Privacy Policy for SelfMaxing AI

Last Updated: October 2026

SelfMaxing AI ("we," "our," or "the App") is committed to protecting your privacy and biometric data. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, process, retain, and protect your personal information across our self-improvement verticals (Hair, Face, Physique, Style, and Communication).

1. Information We Collect

To deliver personalized aesthetic, grooming, and vocal cadence coaching, we collect:

  • Account Details: Email address and Firebase UID (via Google Sign-In or manual authentication).
  • User Media: Images for Hair, Face, Physique, and Style; up to 30-second audio recordings for Communication analysis.
  • Extracted Metrics: Numerical scores, symmetry ratios, seasonal palettes, and daily habit progress.

2. Biometric Privacy & 24-Hour Ephemeral Retention

Ephemeral Processing Guarantee: All user-uploaded photos and audio clips stored in Google Cloud Storage are permanently purged within 24 hours via automated lifecycle rules. Only derived numerical scores and habit checklists are saved to your private profile.

3. AI Processing via Google Gemini

Media inputs are analyzed using secure cloud functions interfacing with Google Gemini API models. Data is processed solely for coaching directives and is never used to train public AI models.

4. Subscriptions & Payments

Payment transactions are processed securely through Google Play Billing and managed via RevenueCat. We never collect or store your payment card or banking credentials.

5. In-App Account & Data Deletion

You can permanently delete your account, metrics history, and all stored data anytime in the app via Settings → Delete Account & Data.

6. Non-Medical Cosmetic Disclaimer

All recommendations, scores, and grooming routines provided by SelfMaxing AI are strictly for aesthetic and self-improvement purposes. We do not provide medical or dermatological advice.

7. Contact Us

For any privacy requests or inquiries, email: support@selfmaxing.ai
