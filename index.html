Privacy Policy for SelfMaxing AI

Last Updated: October 2026 Zero-Storage Architecture

SelfMaxing AI ("we," "our," or "the App") is committed to protecting your privacy and biometric integrity. This Privacy Policy outlines our strict privacy-first practices across our self-improvement verticals (Hair, Face, Physique, Style, and Communication).

1. Information We Collect & Process

To provide personalized appearance and vocal analysis, we handle the following:

  • Account Details: Email address and Firebase User ID (UID) via Google Sign-In or email registration.
  • User Media (Real-Time In-Memory Ingestion): Frontal/profile images for Hair, Face, Physique, and Style; up to 30-second audio clips for Communication coaching.
  • Extracted Coaching Metrics: Structural balance scores, posture alignment grades, seasonal color palettes, and daily routine completion logs stored in Cloud Firestore.

2. Zero-Disk Biometric Privacy Guarantee

Zero Cloud Storage / Real-Time Ephemeral Processing:
SelfMaxing AI operates on a strict zero-storage media model. No user photographs or voice recordings are ever saved to persistent cloud storage, database buckets, or third-party file servers. Media is held ephemerally in volatile device memory (RAM) solely for the duration of inference and is instantly and permanently destroyed upon metric extraction.

3. Generative AI Inference (Google Gemini)

Diagnostic coaching is generated via the Google Gemini API. Media inputs are transmitted via encrypted HTTPS TLS 1.3 streams directly for inference. Media data is never stored by us on disk and is never used to train public foundational AI models.

4. Subscriptions & Payments

Subscription purchases for individual fields and All-Access passes are processed directly by Google Play In-App Billing and managed via RevenueCat. We never store, process, or have access to your credit card, debit card, bank, or UPI details.

5. In-App User Controls & Complete Account Purge

In full compliance with Google Play Store Developer Policies and GDPR/CCPA regulations:

  • Account Deletion: You can permanently wipe your account and all associated habit/metric history anytime directly in the app via Settings → Danger Zone → Delete Account & Data.
  • AI Output Reporting: Users can flag inaccurate or inappropriate AI suggestions directly within the app using the "Report" button on any analysis card.

6. Non-Medical Cosmetic Disclaimer

SelfMaxing AI provides cosmetic grooming, styling, and vocal delivery suggestions for informational, aesthetic, and self-improvement purposes only. None of our analysis constitutes medical, clinical, or dermatological advice.

7. Contact Information

For data privacy requests or support, contact us at: support@selfmaxing.ai
